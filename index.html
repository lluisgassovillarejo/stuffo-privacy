Stuffo — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 June 2026

Stuffo (“the app”) is a manual cash-envelope budgeting app. The short version: everything stays on your device. Stuffo has no account, no server, and no bank connection.

What we collect

Nothing. Stuffo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or financial information. We have no servers and no analytics.

All data you enter — envelope names, budgets, income, and the spending you log — is stored only on your device, in the app’s local database. We never see it and it never leaves your phone unless you choose to back it up yourself (for example, through your own device or Google account backup, which is governed by Google’s policies, not ours).

No bank connection

Stuffo is 100% manual by design. It does not link to your bank, does not use Plaid or any aggregator, does not access your accounts, and does not move money. Every figure in the app is one you typed in yourself.

No accounts

Stuffo has no sign-up and no login. You do not give us a name, email, or phone number to use the app.

Purchases

If Stuffo offers a paid one-time unlock, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment-card details — Google handles billing and only tells the app whether the unlock was purchased. See Google Play’s privacy policy for how Google processes that transaction.

Children

Stuffo is a general-audience budgeting tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no data at all, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Permissions

Stuffo requests no sensitive device permissions (no contacts, location, camera, or SMS).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
